Meet Becky Lee

I’m Becky—founder of Wellspring Writes, where calligraphy, engraving, and foiling aren’t just pretty details, they’re the defining edge of an experience.

My obsession started early: origami, Korean stationery sets, and a fascination with how handwriting carries weight. A calligraphy workshop sparked what’s now become a practice rooted in precision, artistry, and presence. My lifelong love for handwriting and paper found its lane to flourish, and I’ve been building on that ever since.

I’ve partnered with global brands and private clients who understand that small touches are statements. Whether it’s a hand-engraved gift, a live activation, or a bespoke commission, my work is built to stop people in their tracks—elevated, memorable, and never ordinary.

If you want the extra edge, the detail that elevates an experience from nice to unforgettable, you’ve come to the right place.
